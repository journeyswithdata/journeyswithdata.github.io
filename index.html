01

What I actually work on

Most data science writing is about models. Comparatively little is about the messy, regulated, consequential systems models get deployed into — where a wrong number becomes a wrong investment, and a regulator will ask you to show your working. That is the part I find interesting, and it is what I write about.

02

How I work

Four habits that run through everything here. They are less about technique than about making numbers that survive scrutiny.

i

Derive, don’t assert

A count should be the output of a rule you can re-run, not a number someone remembers. Read more →

ii

Make disagreement legible

Systems will disagree. The job is to show where, why and by how much — not to pick a winner quietly. Read more →

iii

Ask how it could be wrong

Find the assumptions that carry the decision, and test them before anyone else does. Read more →

iv

Treat the page as evidence

The reader only has the document. A clipped diagram reads as missing data. Read more →

A few are probably wrong — but hopefully interesting in the attempt.
